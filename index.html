★ HOW TO PLAY

The rules

  • Every professor is one level. Survive their exam and drain their HP bar.
  • Beating a professor gives you 5 points and money. You get a bonus for the HP you have left.
  • Reach 15 points to graduate and win the game.
  • Between levels, visit Office Hours: learn one of two skills from the professor you just beat. The other one is gone forever.
  • Spend leftover money to expand your HP bar and mana bar.
  • Fail an exam? Retake it. Your skills and upgrades are kept.

☠ THE FACULTY

LEVEL 1 · PHYS 101

Prof. Inertia

Mechanics

Drops apples under real gravity, lobs bullets in perfect parabolas, swings a pendulum spray and charges at you with full momentum.

Drops: Momentum Dash or Pendulum Orbit

LEVEL 2 · PHYS 102

Prof. Voltz

Electricity & Magnetism

Calls down lightning columns, fires charges that curl in a magnetic field, plants capacitor mines and blasts Coulomb bursts.

Drops: Chain Lightning or Faraday Cage

LEVEL 3 · PHYS 201

Prof. Amplitude

Waves

Shoots sine-wave streams, sends shockwave rings with a single gap, and sets up standing waves. Hint: stand on the nodes.
